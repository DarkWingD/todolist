Privacy Policy

Kitchen Board for Android

Last updated 3 September 2026

Kitchen Board collects nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you type stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Your meals, weekly plan and shopping list are written to the app's own private storage area on your device. Android keeps that area readable only by Kitchen Board.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no server belonging to us that the app talks to.

What we collect about you

Nothing at all. Specifically, Kitchen Board does not collect, transmit or store:

  • your name, email address or any other identifier
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, files or calendar
  • usage analytics, crash reports or performance data
  • advertising identifiers

There are no third-party SDKs in the app: no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no social logins.

Export and import

Settings offers an Export button, which writes your data to a file and hands it to Android's share sheet so you can save or send it. That is the only way data leaves the device, and it happens only when you tap the button and choose a destination. Where the file goes afterwards is between you and whichever app you picked; we never see it.

Import reads a file you select and replaces what is currently in the app.

Permissions

Kitchen Board requests one Android permission, INTERNET. It is required by the framework the app is built on, which serves the app's own screens from a local address inside the device. The app makes no network requests: you can verify this by using it with the phone in aeroplane mode, where everything works normally.

The app does not request storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Kitchen Board deletes everything it holds, because everything it holds is on your device. You can also clear it from Android's Settings → Apps → Kitchen Board → Storage → Clear data. Export first if you want to keep a copy — once it is gone, we have no backup of it, because we never had a copy in the first place.

Children

Kitchen Board is a meal planner suitable for general audiences. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
